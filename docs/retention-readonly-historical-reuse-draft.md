# Draft: immutable recovery proof + fresh live coverage (read-only)

This increment is for review, **not activation or apply authorization**. The real composition root remains `undefined`; no production key, collector, report signature, restore or operational policy is provisioned here.

Only the independent compiled read-only composition may supply `historicalReuse`: approval of the exact original report/config SHA-256, original seven-digit UTC timestamp and complete `HistoricalOperationalBinding`, plus a separate observer-only `collect` port. Requests, CLI arguments, env and JSON metadata cannot supply that approval. Without the new root field, the original acquisition TTL is unchanged; the executor/apply APIs are unchanged as well.

The read-only-specific verifier checks the original timestamp against the current clock, then replays the unchanged v2 Ed25519, positive collection, target and independently approved SQL/Prisma authentication at the original collection instant. It does not rewrite or re-sign bytes, renew the timestamp, or treat integrity as admission. Wrappers, negative collection, malformed/future dates and mismatched commitments remain refusals.

Historical reuse additionally requires a new live observer: exact artifact scope and complete binding, canonical fresh `checkedAt` (age at most `maxGateAgeMs`), complete per-tag counts and ID **and all original scalar-content** digest sets using the archive encoding. The observer must read the selected live rows, not copy archive/report expectations; the six existing gates alone do not establish this coverage. The observer runs before those gates, and its private observation is rechecked afterward. All six original gate checks, including the complete main/DLQ census, remain required. Approval and original acquisition buffers are copied before awaited work. Each readonly gate observation is deeply copied immediately on its individual completion, before awaiting the other collectors; snapshots are private to each invocation, including concurrent calls.

The decision is finalized only **after all asynchronous cleanup**. The private original acquisition is reauthenticated and the original coverage and all six private gate observations are synchronously revalidated at one final clock reading, without recollecting or renewing timestamps and without any further await before returning the decision. Cleanup failure or external cancellation refuses the diagnostic; ordinary disposal's internal work-signal abort is not itself an external cancellation. The shared gate assertion is factored into a pure function. Readonly wrappers do not grant apply authority. A separate connected durable-authorization increment also snapshots observations in the shared checker used by apply, preserving the assertion and acquisition TTL rules; apply still requires its own independently approved durable ledger, authenticated evidence and real live composition. See `retention-historical-durable-authorization-draft.md` for that separate increment and its unverified operational prerequisites.

Tests use genuinely signed old **synthetic** reports and a separate fixture-row coverage observation; they do not certify SQL, production provenance or production coverage. Current observations do not protect against later writes or replace apply-time concurrency/transaction checks. Before any real activation: independent design/security review, approved real acquisition/catalog provenance and composition, a bounded cancellation-aware live coverage collector, and separately authorized operational gates are still needed. Legacy apply freshness remains a separate policy decision, untouched here.
